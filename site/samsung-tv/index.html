MovieBox for Samsung Tizen TV

A remote-first TV client with a documented developer-install process.

Get Samsung TV bundle v0.9.2Release notes and checksums

Samsung Tizen TV is a separate platform

This is a Samsung Tizen client, not an Android TV APK. It has remote-first browsing, native TV video playback, subtitles, available audio tracks, episode navigation and resume. The app runs independently on the TV after installation.

The published v0.9.2 transport was exercised on a Samsung U8000F running Tizen 9. The project targets Tizen 7 or newer; other models remain unverified. Source-branch work is separate from that published package.

What you need before installing

  • A supported Samsung Tizen TV with Developer Mode enabled.
  • A PC with official Tizen SDK tools and the Samsung TV certificate extension.
  • Your own Samsung TV certificate profile authorized for the target TV's device ID.
  • The complete published installation bundle and its instructions.

There is no universal signed WGT that can be installed on every Samsung TV. Certificates and device authorizations are required by the platform. Keep certificate passwords and private keys local.

Install the published TV bundle

  1. Download and extract the bundle, then read its INSTALL-TV.md.
  2. Confirm the TV's local IP, Developer Mode settings and the correct host PC address.
  3. Use the included helper with your SDK path and authorized certificate profile to sign and install the unsigned widget.
  4. Open MovieBox on the TV and test remote navigation, playback, pause/resume and seeking.

The PC is needed for developer installation, not to host the application during normal TV use. This package is not for LG webOS, Roku, Apple TV or Android TV.

Playback and remote behavior

Auto mode uses the TV HTML video engine for MP4 with application-scoped media request headers. Samsung AVPlay remains available for alternate formats and embedded audio tracks. Arrows, OK, Return and supported transport keys operate the TV interface.

Network interruptions, source formats and model-specific decoders can affect playback. For problems, include the TV model, Tizen version, downloaded release and the actions leading to the failure. Do not share your device ID, certificate files or signed video URLs publicly.
